## Demonstrated remaining scale-zero defect

**Source/device-confirmed, unresolved:** AIDL `CompositeEffect.scale` accepts
0..1, where zero denotes minimum feelable strength, not silence. See the
[Android guidance](https://developer.android.com/develop/ui/views/haptics/custom-haptic-effects).
The current compose validator accepts zero, but the worker's `scale > 0`
condition skips waveform playback and waits out its nominal duration.

A private paired test submits SLOW_RISE with scale 0.25 and 0.0. Both return
success and exactly one completion callback. Read-only custom-wave status
polling observes active playback for 0.25 and none for zero; the zero-scale
playback assertion fails. This confirms the branch behavior, not a measured
perceptibility threshold. Independent review found the same contract mismatch.

No numerical minimum was found in the stock framework/driver/model evidence.
A global gain floor would alter approved low-strength effects and is rejected;
one gain unit is not established as feelable. Preserve the approved positive
scale curve until per-primitive low-scale/quantization measurements and human
perception establish a safe minimum. No guessed tuning fix was committed.
