## Confirmed production defect

A nested `err` in `fastrpc_munmap_on_dsp_rh()` hides failed DSP heap removal.
The caller sees success, including SSR cleanup that otherwise restores the heap
to its tracked list on failure. Remove the shadow and preserve the helper's
actual status in its caller.

The adjacent mapping path overwrites a hypervisor permission-assignment failure
with the DSP rollback result: successful cleanup makes failed mapping appear
successful. Keep the original assignment error and log rollback errors separately.
Read/publish the initial mapping RPC output only after that RPC succeeds.
Successful operation, permissions, native/compat layout and the existing
unlock/relock order are preserved. Human-authored upstream commits are unchanged.

The comparison [Qualcomm source](https://android.googlesource.com/kernel/msm.git/+/7234d8527f6dfeb3e828c1e53e4b11a18d1aabe2/drivers/char/adsprpc.c)
contains the older shadowed implementation; this is provenance, not a claim that
the local fix imports a verified upstream replacement.

## Warning baseline and limits

Final corpus: **3,883 distinct C sources**, **150,176 parsed
unique primary warnings**, **4,537,295 occurrences**, including repeated
headers. Conversion, layout/padding and qualifier warnings dominate. This large
baseline is not fully classified or fixed; a W=2/3 report alone is not proof of
a runtime defect. Kernel-doc reports are separate from these compiler counts.

Reviewed unchanged examples: scoped DMA/KGSL failure cursors, Wi-Fi statuses
with immediate error returns, exact `(2*h)-1` fractional clock encoding,
zsmalloc's four stored fullness states versus its sentinel, and a 16 KiB
CRC-input bound. No layouts/casts were changed merely to quiet warnings.
The wider sparse backlog was not rerun and documentation was not changed.
