# Release artwork

Artwork summarizes the [decided release delta](android16-android17-release-state.md); it must not invent features or turn a pending test into a fix.

## Current release banner direction

The latest supplied skeleton supersedes the older crimson/industrial series for this release: blue/navy 16:9 mountain composition, white Evolution branding with blue X, title/changelog panels on the left, overlapping front/rear Arctic White phones on the right, date upper right and compact footer cards.

September 27 draft labels: Android 16 (QPR-2), Evolution X v11.11, 27.09.2026. Artwork is not publication evidence. Use release-ledger features and supplied installation guidance; never reuse old download URLs. Keep main sections to 2-4 readable bullets.

Match real POCO F3 hardware: large lenses at top and bottom of the black vertical pill, small optical lens at middle-left and textured non-lens circle beside it; flash and dot on the silver strip to the right. Three rear optical cameras, not four. [Official specs](https://www.po.co/global/poco-f3/specs) list 48 MP main, 8 MP ultrawide and 5 MP telemacro; [product photo](https://www.f-mobil.cz/telefon/poco-f3-8gb-256gb-arctic-white) establishes the visible arrangement. Front camera is a centered punch-hole. Custom ROM 4K60 claims require project evidence.

Reference and generated banner are conversation artifacts, not bundled here. Retrieve the exact image only when an edit needs it. Approval of the regenerated banner is not recorded.

## Avatars and acceptance

Square group/GitHub art must retain POCO F3 identity and read at small size; detailed release cards are not suitable unchanged. Use generous edge/corner clearance for rounded previews and match the requested crop. Current user artwork instructions take precedence over series defaults.

Inspect exact date/version, truthful current highlights, English spelling, alignment, margins, phone hardware, display/background consistency and functional architecture. Reject old-release leftovers, unsupported feature claims, malformed cameras, pseudo-text and clutter. Source prompts and longer edit/generation examples remain available in [the original artwork record](../archive/2026-09-26/memory/release-assets.md), not in every startup prompt. Historical artwork-specific imports are indexed in [the archive](../archive/README.md).
