# Release artwork

Artwork summarizes the [initial-release changelogs](../state/releases.md). Use the
final [large](../state/telegram-initial-full.md) and [small](../state/telegram-initial-short.md)
posts as copy sources; compress them into readable highlights without inventing claims.
Technical acceptance evidence remains in validation, separate from Telegram copy.

## Android 17 style — mandatory

Use an Arknights-inspired industrial/dystopian composition and a red-and-black
palette: dark city towers, angular tactical panels, restrained red lights and
accents, white readable typography and a red Evolution X accent. Both Arknights
styling and red/black are required for this series. The supplied September 22
banner is the visual reference, not current release metadata or feature evidence.

Default composition: landscape 16:9, overlapping front/rear black POCO F3 phones
on the left, Evolution X/device title above organized changelog panels on the
right. Match the front-screen wallpaper to the scene. Keep 2–4 concise bullets
per panel, generous spacing and readable text at Telegram preview size. Avoid
clutter, excessive glow and tiny technical paragraphs.

Use Android 17 and POCO F3 (alioth); include Redmi K40 / Mi 11X when space allows.
Add version, build date, installation instructions, download/support links only
from current supplied release details. Do not reuse September 22, v12.2, PayPal
or clean-install wording merely because it appears in the reference image.
The former blue/navy Android 16 mountain direction is not this series default.

## POCO F3 camera geometry — mandatory

Match the supplied phone reference and official product photography. The rear
camera island contains exactly three optical cameras and a separate microphone:

- One large circular camera at the top.
- One smaller/medium optical camera at middle-left.
- One circular microphone at middle-right, immediately beside that camera.
  Preserve its visible round surround/rim and relative radius from the reference;
  show its dark, non-glass textured center. It must not become a fourth lens,
  vanish, shrink to an unrelated pinhole or merge into the neighboring camera.
- One circular camera at the bottom.

Keep the vertical rounded black pill, metallic edging, spacing and perspective
consistent. Preserve the separate elongated flash on the outer right-hand strip
and the centered front-camera punch-hole. Never mirror the internal arrangement.
Do not invent millimeter measurements: the reference controls relative radii.

Official specifications confirm 48 MP main, 8 MP ultrawide and 5 MP telemacro;
the product page documents the triple-microphone system. These do not establish
custom-ROM 4K60 support; that claim comes from project validation.

## Generation and acceptance

1. Read current release copy and metadata; retrieve the supplied image when needed.
2. Use the mandatory style, composition and camera geometry in the generation prompt.
   For an edit, include the actual reference image and preserve untargeted elements.
3. Inspect the result at full size and Telegram preview size: three optical cameras,
   correct microphone circle/radius/texture, flash, punch-hole, spelling, branding,
   date/version, margins, contrast and truthful highlights.
4. Regenerate or edit incorrect hardware or text before delivering the banner.

Square group/GitHub avatars need a simpler crop with generous clearance for rounded
previews. Detailed release panels do not transfer unchanged to avatar artwork.
User artwork instructions take precedence over defaults. Reference images and
renders are not bundled here; this guide records conventions, not a new render.
